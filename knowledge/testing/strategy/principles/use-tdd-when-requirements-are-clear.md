## Rule

Write the test first when the requirements are clear: pure functions, bug fixes, UIs with a well-defined
design. In exploratory coding, add the tests once the direction has settled.

## Why

Tests written first during exploration get rewritten with every change of direction, and tests postponed
on clear requirements give up the fastest feedback available.
