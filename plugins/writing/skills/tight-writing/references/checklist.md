# Checklist

Run after writing or editing. Answer each item pass or fail; fix every fail and run the list
again. For Review mode, run only the last section.

## Sense and voice

1. The core point is in the first sentence or two.
2. No fact, number, source, quote or event appears that is not in the conversation, the draft or a
   source actually read. Missing facts are asked for or shown as placeholders.
3. Opinions are marked as opinions. In Edit mode, every added fact or opinion is listed under
   **What changed**.
4. The writer would recognise the text as their own: vocabulary, rhythm and tone survive.

## Brevity

5. No sentence can be deleted without losing information.
6. No point is made twice.
7. No warm-up opener, announcement of what follows, recap or filler courtesy.
8. Length fits the channel.
9. Every sentence passes the portability test.

## Consistency

10. Each concept has one name throughout.
11. Numbers, dates, names and amounts match wherever they repeat.
12. The ending agrees with the opening; no caveat cancels the main claim.
13. Tone does not shift partway through.
14. Any conflict that could not be resolved is flagged to the user, not silently decided.

## Patterns and format

15. No stock words or empty phrases from `patterns.md`, unless quoted as examples.
16. No false contrasts, teaser setups, colon reveals, inflated significance, vague attribution or
    mic-drop endings.
17. Formatting matches the length: prose for short text, no decorative bold or emoji, dashes
    rare.

## Output

18. Write: the text only. Edit: the full text plus a short **What changed** list. Review: one line
    per finding with pattern name, quoted text and fix, and no rewrite, score or AI-authorship
    guess.
